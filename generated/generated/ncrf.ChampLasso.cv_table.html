ncrf.ChampLasso.cv_table¶

ChampLasso.cv_table(cv_results)¶

Summarize cross-validation scores by mu.

Call this on the solver that was selected by search(); the table marks it among the candidates it was chosen from. The table also warns when its mu is at the bottom of the grid it was chosen from.

Parameters:

cv_results – The results the selection was made from.
